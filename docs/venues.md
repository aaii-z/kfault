# kfault — Target Venues (shortlist)

Three realistic homes for the "silent failures in LLM-generated GPU kernels"
work, given an **end-of-August 2026** finish. Costs are **approximate** —
confirm on each site before committing. GBP figures assume ~£1 = $1.27.

---

## 1. EuroMLSys 2027 — *top pick (UK/Europe, on-topic)*

Workshop on Machine Learning and Systems, co-located with EuroSys. Exact venue
for the field. (The 2026 edition was in Edinburgh, UK, April 2026 — deadline
already passed, so target **2027**.)

- **Format:** short **workshop paper** — up to **6 pages** (10pt, two-column,
  SIGPLAN template), references excluded from the limit. Single-blind. Poster
  session included.
- **Deadline:** ~**Jan–Feb 2027** (CFP opens late 2026). Fits an Aug finish +
  autumn polish.
- **Location:** rotates around Europe (EuroSys 2027 TBD; may not be UK).
- **Cost (approx.):** workshop-only student registration typically
  **~£150–£300**, plus travel. Check the EuroSys 2027 registration page.
- Links: https://euromlsys.eu/ · https://2026.eurosys.org/

## 2. NeurIPS 2026 Workshop — *best timing, highest visibility (not UK)*

Submit to a relevant accepted workshop (e.g. Deep Learning for Code / ML for
Systems / Reliable ML).

- **Format:** short **workshop paper** — typically **4–8 pages** (per-workshop),
  non-archival, poster + possible spotlight.
- **Deadline:** contribution submissions open ~**end Aug / Sept 2026** — lines
  up with your finish date.
- **Location:** Sydney / Paris / Atlanta, Dec 2026 (in-person).
- **Cost (approx.):** student registration **~$375–$500** (2026 TBD), plus
  significant travel/accommodation. Financial-assistance grants exist.
- Links: https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/CallForPapers

## 3. UK showcase poster (Turing-style / CDT symposia) — *low-bar first outing*

E.g. Alan Turing Institute showcase events ("AI, Engineering Biology and
Beyond"-family), your CDT/faculty symposia.

- **Format:** **poster + short abstract** (~1 page). Not peer-reviewed
  publication — visibility, not a citation.
- **Deadline:** varies (abstracts often ~1–2 months before the event).
- **Location:** UK, in-person.
- **Cost (approx.):** often **free or low (£0–£100)** for accepted poster
  presenters; some events charge modest registration. Check per event.
- Links: https://www.turing.ac.uk/events

**Plan:** aim the polished 6-page paper at **EuroMLSys 2027**; submit the same
short paper to a **NeurIPS 2026 workshop** in parallel (timing fits); use a
